§ 05 / Rules

What keeps it coherent

Rule 01

Blueprints are for machines

Cyan + grid only on technical surfaces: die plan, wafer map, tech tree, process. Never on financials or news.

Rule 02

Numbers are always mono

Tabular figures, right-aligned, unit in a dimmed span. If it can be compared down a column, it is mono.

Rule 03

Colour never alone

Every gain/loss carries ▲▼ and a sign. Colourblind palettes swap the three accents without touching layout.
